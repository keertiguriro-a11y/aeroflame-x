NASA partial-gravity reality

While NASA targets Artemis lunar bases (0.16g) and Martian habitats (0.38g), direct partial-gravity combustion data is limited in the PSI database. Researchers bridge this gap using 0g/1g bounding and electric-field (ACME E-FIELD) ion dynamics.

How this app handles it: 0.16 g and 0.38 g results interpolate between 0 g and 1 g behavior (buoyant flow scaled with the square root of g) and carry lower confidence than the 0 g cases.

ISS hardware map

  • FLEX / FLEX-2 / CFI: Multi-user Droplet Combustion Apparatus (MDCA) in the Combustion Integrated Rack (CIR).
  • BASS / BASS-II / SPICE: Microgravity Science Glovebox (MSG) with fan-driven flow ducts.
  • Saffire-I to VI: uncrewed Cygnus vehicle, large-scale burns.
  • ACME E-FIELD: electric-field flame control studies in the CIR.
